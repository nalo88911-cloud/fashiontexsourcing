WELCOME TO OUR COMPANY

A GLOBAL SOURCING GROUP

Fashion Tex is a creative sourcing and solution provider in the Asian Market. Our innovative, cost-efficient supply chain and professional facilities serve and contribute towards the success of our customers in the sourcing market. We are an industry-leading one-stop-solution with roots in Asian and European traditions, committed to providing all our clients the highest level of service.

Our vertically integrated operations, diversified product range, and our people make us committed to move thousands of products every year all around the world. The German tradition and European expertise in design and quality fused with a strong setup in Asia makes us respond to the highest sourcing and production demands all over the world.
